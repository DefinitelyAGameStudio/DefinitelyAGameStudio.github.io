Grimglade

Pick a hero, grab upgrades and build a combo that melts the horde. The bosses keep coming, and every ten minutes the Reaper does too.

Google Play: closed test from Sep 30 itch.io PC build: soon

How a run goes

There is no finish line. Your score is how long you survive, then how many monsters you took down.

  1. 0:00
    Pick a hero and two core traitsSix heroes, each with their own starting weapon and passive.
  2. 3:00
    The Black KnightThe first boss. Learn to read the red telegraphs.
  3. 6:00
    The Frost ColossusTougher, slower, and it hits like a glacier.
  4. 10:00
    The ForgottenSeals every weapon except your starter. Break its pylons to win them back.
  5. 13:00
    A new boss every three minutesEach one brings a counter rule, so the same build won't beat them all.
  6. 20:00
    The ReaperIt can't be hurt. Complete its task to banish it, then it returns every ten minutes.

What's in it

Builds that stack

Weapons evolve, relics stack without limit, and element set bonuses reward going all in on fire, frost or lightning.

Gamble for power

Chest slot machines, cursed chests, pacts that trade a weakness for strength, and gamble shrines that raise the stakes.

Trials mid-run

Every few minutes the game throws a mini-mode at you: Gauntlet, Defend the Crystal, Bullet Storm, Closing Ring and more.

Readable danger

Every big enemy attack is telegraphed on the ground. Deaths are on you, and you learn from them.
