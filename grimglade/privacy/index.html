Grimglade privacy policy

Effective September 25, 2026

This policy covers Grimglade on Android and Windows, made by Definitely A Real Studio (an independent developer). In short: the game does not collect, store on a server or share any personal data.

What the game collects

Nothing. Grimglade has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting services and no third-party tracking tools. The Android version does not request internet access, so it cannot send data anywhere.

What stays on your device

The game saves these files locally on your device so you can keep playing:

  • your progress: unlocked heroes, coins, quests and upgrades;
  • your settings, such as volume, screen shake and camera zoom;
  • a history of your finished runs, used for your personal best times and stats.

These files never leave your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Permissions

On Android the game asks for one permission: vibration, used for haptic feedback. You can turn vibration off in the game's options.

Google Play

If you download the game from Google Play, Google handles the download and may collect data under its own policies. See the Google privacy policy.

Children

Grimglade is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future update adds online features, such as leaderboards, this policy will be updated on this page before that update is released. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/DefinitelyAGameStudio/DefinitelyAGameStudio.github.io/issues.
