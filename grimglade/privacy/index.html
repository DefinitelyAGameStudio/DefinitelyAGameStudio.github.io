Grimglade privacy policy

Effective September 25, 2026

This policy covers Grimglade on Android and Windows, made by Definitely A Real Studio (an independent developer). In short: there are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The only data that leaves your device is your leaderboard entry on the Windows version, and only after you choose a leaderboard name.

Android version

The Android version collects nothing. It does not request internet access, so it cannot send data anywhere.

Windows version: online leaderboard

The Windows version has an online leaderboard run by SilentWolf, a leaderboard service for games. The first time a run could go on the leaderboard, the game asks you to choose a name. If you cancel, nothing is sent. Once you have chosen a name, the game sends your new best runs to SilentWolf. For each one it sends:

  • the name you chose (don't use your real name if you'd rather not);
  • your score: the time you survived and your kills;
  • a summary of the run: level, hero, weapons, bosses and Reapers beaten, difficulty and curses, how the run ended, the game version, and whether the run passed the game's anti-cheat checks;
  • a random ID the game creates on first use, so the leaderboard can tell your entries apart from someone else's with the same name. It is not linked to your device or any account.

When you open the leaderboard screen, the game downloads the top scores. Like any website, SilentWolf receives your IP address and the time of each request. Leaderboard entries are public: anyone playing the game can see names and scores. To have your entry removed, contact us (see below).

What stays on your device

The game saves these files locally on your device so you can keep playing:

  • your progress: unlocked heroes, coins, quests and upgrades;
  • your settings, such as volume, screen shake and camera zoom;
  • a history of your finished runs, used for your personal best times and stats;
  • on Windows, your leaderboard name and the random ID described above.

Apart from the leaderboard entries described above, these files never leave your device. Uninstalling the game or deleting its save folder deletes them.

Permissions

On Android the game asks for one permission: vibration, used for haptic feedback. You can turn vibration off in the game's options.

Google Play

If you download the game from Google Play, Google handles the download and may collect data under its own policies. See the Google privacy policy.

Children

Grimglade is not directed at children under 13. It does not ask for real names, email addresses or any other contact details.

Changes

If a future update adds more online features, such as leaderboards on Android, this policy will be updated on this page before that update is released. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/DefinitelyAGameStudio/DefinitelyAGameStudio.github.io/issues.
